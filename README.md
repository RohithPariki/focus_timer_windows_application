# ⏳ Focus Planner Clock

A modern productivity desktop app designed for students and professionals to manage time, tasks, and focus — all in one place

## ✨ Features

- 🕒 **Live Digital Clock**  
- 🍅 **Pomodoro Timer** (25/5 productivity cycles)  
- ✅ **To-Do List** with save/edit/delete and filter options  
- ⏰ **Alarm System** with sound alerts  
- ⌛ **Countdown Timer** (e.g., till exams or deadlines)  
- 🌤️ **Weather Widget** (live weather for your city)  
- 🗓️ **Calendar Viewer** (monthly overview)  
- 📝 **Notes Section**  
- 📚 **Study Tracker** (log time, subjects, and progress)

> All data is saved locally, so your tasks and progress remain intact.
